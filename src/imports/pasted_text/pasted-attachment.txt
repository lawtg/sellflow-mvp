# SELLFLOW — FIGMA MASTER DESIGN & PRODUCT ENVIRONMENT

## ROLE

Act as a **senior SaaS product designer, UX strategist, UI designer, and design-systems architect**.

You are designing the complete product environment for **SellFlow**, an AI-powered platform that helps African digital-product creators turn their digital products into professional online sales systems.

This design will later be handed to an AI coding agent such as **Kiro** to implement.

Your job at this stage is **NOT to write backend code or build the actual application logic**.

Your job is to create the **complete visual, UX, interaction, component, and design-system foundation** that a developer can implement accurately.

---

# 1. PRODUCT

## Working Product Name

**SellFlow**

The name may change later, so do not make the entire visual identity dependent on the wordmark.

## Core Positioning

SellFlow is not simply a platform for uploading and selling PDFs.

It is an **AI-powered digital product sales system**.

The core promise:

> **Tell us what you're selling. We'll help you build the sales system to sell it.**

Alternative positioning:

> Create. Sell. Deliver. Grow.

The product should feel like the easiest place for a creator to go from:

**Digital product → Professional sales page → Checkout → Payment → Delivery → Customers → Follow-up**

# 3. THE CORE USER EXPERIENCE

The primary experience should communicate:

> **"I have something to sell. SellFlow will help me turn it into something people can buy."**

Ideal flow:

1. Sign up
2. Enter dashboard
3. Click **Create Product**
4. Upload PDF
5. SellFlow understands the product
6. AI asks a few intelligent questions
7. AI generates a professional sales page
8. Creator reviews and edits it
9. Creator connects payment
10. Creator publishes
11. Customer visits sales page
12. Customer checks out
13. Payment is processed
14. Customer receives download
15. Creator sees the sale in dashboard

The product should make this journey visually obvious.

# 49. FINAL DESIGN QUALITY BAR

Before completing the design, review the entire product as if you were a creator who has never used SellFlow.

Ask:

### Can I understand what SellFlow does within 5 seconds?

### Can I create my first product without instructions?

### Do I understand what the AI is doing?

### Does the AI feel useful rather than decorative?

### Can I clearly see how to get my product published?

### Does the sales page editor feel easier than building a website?

### Does checkout feel trustworthy?

### Does the dashboard make sense immediately?

### Does the entire product feel premium?

### Does the experience feel significantly easier than using a collection of separate tools?

If any answer is no, improve the design.
